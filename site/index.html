About Me

Hi, I'm Thi — founder of The 25th

With over 8 years of experience in the beauty industry, I specialize in creating customized lash and brow services designed to enhance your natural beauty while maintaining the health of your natural lashes and brows.

My passion extends beyond beauty services — I also love educating and mentoring aspiring lash artists, helping them build strong foundations in retention, isolation, styling, and lash artistry. My goal is a luxury experience where every client feels confident, comfortable, and beautiful.

Whether you're looking for a natural enhancement, wispy lashes, anime-inspired styles, a lash lift, or perfectly styled brows, every service is tailored to your unique features and goals.
